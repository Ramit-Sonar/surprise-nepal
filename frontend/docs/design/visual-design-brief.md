==================================================
7. SHADOW SYSTEM
==================================================

Shadows are one of the MOST IMPORTANT parts of this redesign.

Use multiple subtle layers rather than one harsh shadow.

Raised component:
- Soft dark ambient shadow
- Very soft light highlight
- Large blur
- Low opacity
- Natural depth

Pressed component:
- Inner/directional shadow
- Subtle opposite highlight
- Visually appears pushed into the surface

Every shadow must feel:
- Soft
- Diffused
- Natural
- Lightweight
- Physical

NEVER use:
- Black heavy shadows
- Sharp shadows
- Large dramatic shadows
- Excessive elevation

The reference image should be treated as the visual benchmark for shadow softness.

==================================================
8. BORDER RADIUS
==================================================

Use a consistent radius system.

Large containers:
approximately 24–32px

Cards:
approximately 20–28px

Buttons:
approximately 18–28px depending on size

Inputs:
approximately 16–24px

Small controls:
approximately 12–18px

Do NOT make every element excessively pill-shaped.

Use rounded geometry according to the component's purpose.

Secondary buttons:

- Subtle shadow

Icon buttons:
- Circular or softly rounded
- Raised surface
- Centered icon
- Consistent dimensions

- Reduced shadow
